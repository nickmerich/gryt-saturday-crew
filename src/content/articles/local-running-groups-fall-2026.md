Local running groups range from free, drop-in Saturday crews to structured programs with coached workouts. The seven groups below include a few specific clubs and a few national networks you can find in many cities, so you can compare options by location, pace, meeting day, and cost this fall.

## Why Join a Local Running Group This Fall?

A running group gives you something a solo plan can't: a standing appointment with people who show up whether you feel like it or not.

There's some evidence it helps performance too. A [2024 PLOS One study of more than 200,000 London Marathon runners](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0306853) found that running club membership was associated with faster finishing times, with improvements of up to 40 minutes in some groups. That's an association in marathoners, not proof that a club will make you faster, but accountability and company help at every level.

Group running is also growing. Strava's [2024 Year in Sport report](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-annual-year-in-sport-trend) found a 59% increase in running club participation globally.

## What to Look for in a Running Group

**Location.** A group two miles from your house beats a better one across town. If you have to plan around the drive, you'll eventually skip it.

**Schedule.** Saturday mornings are common. Weeknight runs suit people who race on weekends or prefer evenings.

**Cost.** Many groups are free. Some charge for coached programs or membership. Know what you're paying for.

**Social fit.** No-drop groups keep everyone together. Pace-sorted groups split into faster and slower packs. Check whether people hang around for coffee after. That's often where the crew forms.

## 1. Koreatown Run Club, Los Angeles

Koreatown Run Club started in April 2016 with about 20 people and now draws hundreds at a time, roughly 800 to a thousand runners across a typical week according to co-founder Duy Nguyen, per [a 2026 LAist profile](https://laist.com/brief/news/los-angeles-activities/koreatown-run-club-la). At that size, you can show up on week one, find people at your pace, and build from there. Check the club's social channels for current meeting times and routes.

## 2. Philly Run Tribe, Philadelphia

Philly Run Tribe began in summer 2024 when five friends met for a nighttime run, and it grew quickly through social media, according to [6abc](https://6abc.com/videoClip/19858901/). The group typically meets Tuesday evenings on Kelly Drive. A weeknight evening option suits people who work early or race on weekends. Check the club's Instagram for the current location before you go.

## 3. November Project, Many Cities

November Project runs free, early-morning workout gatherings in cities across North America and beyond. Workouts mix running, stairs, hills, and bodyweight exercises, and the motto is "just show up." Times vary by city. [November Project Baltimore](https://november-project.com/baltimore-md/), for example, meets Wednesday and Friday mornings with no sign-up and no cost. Look up your city's page for current times and meeting spots.

## 4. parkrun, More Than 100 U.S. Events

parkrun holds free, timed 5Ks on Saturday mornings, and [parkrun USA lists more than 100 events](https://www.parkrun.us/). You register once, bring your barcode, and walk, jog, or run. There's no time limit, and a volunteer tail walker always finishes last. In Pittsburgh, [Three Rivers Heritage Trail parkrun](https://www.parkrun.us/threeriversheritagetrail/) runs every Saturday at 9:00 AM on the North Shore. The timed format gives you a free weekly benchmark through the fall.

## 5. Black Girls RUN!, Many Cities

Black Girls RUN! has local groups in cities across the country, each organized through a Facebook group run by local ambassadors. Joining is free, per its [Find a Community page](https://blackgirlsrun.com/find-a-community/). Groups welcome every experience level, from first-timers to race trainers. Check your local group for meeting times.

## 6. Running Store Run Clubs, Many Cities

Many specialty running stores host free weekly group runs. Fleet Feet stores, for example, often list [free social runs where every pace is welcome](https://www.fleetfeet.com/s/mountpleasant/fleet-feet-running-club/MondayRunday), sometimes with shorter and longer route options. In the Pittsburgh area, Fleet Feet Pittsburgh runs a Tuesday 6:30 PM South Hills Pub Run from Arthur's Korner Pub in Mt. Lebanon and a Sunday 7:30 AM long run. For a comparison of store clubs and independent clubs, see [Fleet Feet Pittsburgh vs independent run clubs](/resources/playbooks/fleet-feet-pittsburgh-vs-independent-run-clubs).

## 7. GRYT Run Club, McMurray, PA

[GRYT Run Club](/) meets every Saturday at 7:00 AM at 212 Valley Brook Road in McMurray, PA, on the Montour/Peters Trail, a flat, paved rail trail. The route is about 4 miles out and back, and you can turn around early. Walk, jog, or run. It's no-drop: the run ends when the last person finishes, and faster runners loop back. It's free, with no membership, fees, or pace requirement. First-timers RSVP on the website and sign a short liability waiver. There's coffee in the parking lot after.

## How to Find a Local Running Group Not on This List

**Check club directories.** The [RRCA club finder](https://www.rrca.org/clubs/) lists member clubs by state, and many include contact links.

**Search Instagram and Facebook.** Try your city name plus "run club." If a group posted in the last two weeks, it's active. If the last post is from months ago, move on.

**Ask at a running store.** Staff almost always know which groups meet nearby.

**Ask at a race.** After the finish, ask the runners around you where they train. A personal invite goes a long way.

## FAQs

### What pace do I need to join a running group as a beginner?

For no-drop and all-paces groups, any pace works, including walking. parkrun has no time limit, and GRYT Run Club welcomes walkers, joggers, and runners. Start toward the back and let consistency do the rest.

### Do local running groups require you to sign up in advance?

Many free groups don't. parkrun asks you to register once to get a barcode, and some clubs, including GRYT, ask first-timers to RSVP and sign a waiver. Coached programs usually require registration. Check the group's page before your first visit.

### Are there good fall races to train for with a group?

Yes. Many groups train together for local fall and holiday races. In Pittsburgh, the EQT Pittsburgh 10 Miler is Sunday, November 8, 2026, and several Thanksgiving 5Ks are on November 26, 2026. See the [Pittsburgh races page](/races) for more.

### How do running groups handle bad weather?

Most groups run in light rain and cold, and cancel for lightning, ice, or dangerous heat. Check the group's social media the morning of if the weather looks uncertain.

## Conclusion

Running groups work because they turn "should I run today?" into "where are we meeting?" The groups above range from huge city crews to a free Saturday run in suburban Pittsburgh.

Pick the group closest to you and show up once. Arrive a few minutes early and introduce yourself to whoever is near the start. That first run tells you more than any website.
