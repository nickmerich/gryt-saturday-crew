If your long run is the Saturday out-and-back from McMurray, the National Tunnel closure does not touch it. If you were going to run through the tunnel to connect the west and east sides of the Montour, it does.

Those are different days. Mixing them up is how people drive an hour and find a fence.

## What the council says

The [Montour Trail Council](https://montourtrail.org/) posted this on the trail homepage:

National Tunnel, mile marker 25, is closed indefinitely. During an inspection in late 2025, engineers found deteriorated concrete in the ceiling, enough to affect the tunnel's stability. The structure was closed for initial stabilization. Contractors estimated the repair at $3.2 million.

The council does not recommend an on-road detour. The reason they give is narrow lanes and limited sight lines. Do not invent a road bypass because a map app draws a straight line.

If you still want to be on the trail on either side of the closure:

- Heading west, get on at the McConnell Road trailhead, milepost 24.7.
- Heading east, get on at Kurnick, milepost 25.6.

That is the official split. It is not a suggestion to run the road between them.

[TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/montour-trail/) also carries a closure notice and describes the system as 63 miles, mostly crushed limestone, with paved sections in Peters Township and Clairton. An older note on that page about freezer doors keeping the tunnel above freezing describes how the tunnel used to be managed in winter. It is not a reason to enter a closed structure. The council's current notice wins.

## What this does not change on Saturday

GRYT still meets at 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray, at 7:00 AM. The saved route is 4.2 miles, 81 feet, out and back on the paved Peters section. The National Tunnel is at mile marker 25. That is not our lot, and it is not the bridge turnaround.

You can still turn at 1 mile, 2 miles, or go long on the trail that leaves from the sign. Most people do 3 to 6 miles. Some go 8 or more. None of that requires the tunnel.

The [distance guide](/resources/articles/saturday-distance-from-mcmurray) is the page for that decision. The [winter note](/resources/articles/montour-winter-saturday) is the page for ice, darkness, and the morning-of call.

## How to plan a longer day without the tunnel

Pick a trailhead on the side you actually want, and run out-and-back from there. Do not plan a through-run that depends on the tunnel.

A useful longer day from our side of the system stays on the Peters pavement and the limestone beyond it, with a turnaround you chose. [Peters Township](https://www.peterstownship.com/Facilities/Facility/Details/Arrowhead-Trail-7) describes 3.5 miles of paved Arrowhead Trail from Route 19 to Brush Run Road. That is a known paved piece. Past it, expect crushed limestone, not the same footing as the Saturday route.

If you want the west side, start at McConnell Road and run away from the closure, not toward a detour the council told you not to take.

Bring water if you will be out longer than 45 minutes. Tell someone your turnaround. The Montour is long enough to add miles by accident.

## Why the repair number is in here

$3.2 million is not a rumor from a comment thread. It is the council's own estimate on the trail homepage. It is also the reason "indefinitely" is the right word. Do not build a spring long-run plan that assumes the tunnel reopens on a date nobody has published.

Check [montourtrail.org](https://montourtrail.org/) the week you go. This article was checked on November 17, 2026. A closure this size can change. The Saturday lot will not.

## How far mile marker 25 is from a Saturday

Mile markers on the Montour are trail miles, not driving miles from McMurray. Marker 25 is the tunnel. Our lot is on the Peters Township section, the paved Arrowhead piece that TrailLink places after the trail passes under US 19. You do not stumble into the tunnel on a 4-mile Saturday. You would have to be trying to cross the system.

That is the mistake to avoid. A person hears "Montour Trail," opens a map, and draws a line through the tunnel because the line looks continuous. The council already broke that line. Plan the side you can enter. Leave the other side for another day.

If a mapping app still draws you through the tunnel, the app is wrong. The council page is the alert. Screenshot it before you drive if you are meeting people who will trust the app over you.

## If you only wanted a longer Saturday

Stay in McMurray. Run out. Turn around. Come back to the sign.

The tunnel is a trip. Saturday is a habit. Do not skip the habit because a tunnel 25 miles along the system is closed.

## FAQs

**Is the Saturday run cancelled because the tunnel is closed?**
No. The Saturday run does not go through the National Tunnel.

**Can I run the road around the tunnel?**
The Montour Trail Council does not recommend an on-road detour, because of narrow lanes and limited sight lines. Use McConnell Road or Kurnick instead, and stay on the trail on that side.

**When will it reopen?**
The council says indefinitely. The repair estimate on their site is $3.2 million. There is no published reopen date as of November 17, 2026.

**Where do I check before a long day?**
https://montourtrail.org/ for alerts. For a GRYT Saturday, Instagram or Strava the morning of, only if ice or lightning is the question.
