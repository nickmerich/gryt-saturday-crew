Strava does not publish a top 10 of Pittsburgh running routes. Segment Explore shows a sample of segments near wherever you zoom, and that sample changes. Athlete counts sit behind a login. So this is not a leaderboard.

It is the list we would hand someone who asked where to run. Every distance below comes from a trail page, a city page, or the route we already save for Saturday. If a number is not here, we did not have a source we trusted.

The photo is our Saturday route, the same map on the homepage. 4.2 miles, 81 feet of gain, out and back on the Montour Trail from Valley Brook Road.

## Saturday route on the Montour Trail

This is the one in the photo. On Strava it is named GRYT RUN CLUB (Valleybrook CC Bridge + Back). It is 4.2 miles with 81 feet of elevation gain. You run out, turn around, and finish together.

Park at 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray, PA 15317. Free gravel lot at the trailhead, room for about 20 cars. Meet by the trail map board.

[Open the route on Strava](https://www.strava.com/routes/3538247257394386560)

GRYT runs this every Saturday at 7:00 AM. Walk, jog, or run. The run is no-drop, which means it ends when the last person finishes and faster people loop back. It is free. No membership is required. First-timers RSVP on grytrunclub.com and sign a short waiver. Coffee is in the lot after.

We meet in McMurray, not downtown. The North Shore and Frick Park are good runs. They are not the Saturday meetup.

## Arrowhead Trail

The Saturday turnaround sits on the paved Peters Township section. [Peters Township](https://www.peterstownship.com/Facilities/Facility/Details/Arrowhead-Trail-7) describes the Arrowhead Trail as 3.5 miles of paved trail from Route 19 to Brush Run Road. If 4.2 miles out and back is short, keep going on that paved stretch instead of turning at the bridge.

More South Hills trail notes are in the [rail trails guide](/resources/articles/south-hills-rail-trails-running).

## Eliza Furnace Trail

The city calls this the connector from Schenley Park to Downtown. [The National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/places/eliza-furace-trail.htm) describes a six-mile out-and-back, flat and paved the whole way, from the southwest corner of Schenley Park to Point State Park and back. Runners still call it the Jail Trail because it passes the county jail.

It is useful more than it is pretty. The highway is right there. It is still the car-free line between Oakland and downtown.

## North Shore

[The Three Rivers Heritage Trail page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Rivers_Heritage_Trail) says the trail extends three miles up the north side of the Allegheny River to Millvale. That is the stretch past the ballpark and the stadium. Flat, paved, and busy on game days.

The same page says the trail also runs three miles down the north bank of the Ohio toward Brunot Island if you want the other direction.

[Pennsylvania DCNR](https://www.trails.dcnr.pa.gov/trails/trail/trailview?trailkey=189) lists the whole system at 25.93 miles. Wikipedia's overview says about 33 miles. They are counting the pieces differently. Use the segment lengths when you are picking a run, not the system total.

## South Side riverfront

Wikipedia says the trail goes five miles upriver from Station Square to a point just short of the Waterfront. That is the South Side stretch. Keep going and you are headed toward the Great Allegheny Passage.

[The city trails page](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/Recreation-Events/CitiParks/Outdoor-Activities/Trails) groups the South Side Trail with the North Shore as the skyline stretches.

## Washington's Landing

[TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/washingtons-landing-trail/) lists this at 2.1 miles of crushed stone. It forms almost a loop around the island in the Allegheny and ties into the Heritage Trail at River Avenue, under the 31st Street Bridge. A good short run when you do not want a long out-and-back.

## North Park Lake Trail

[North Park's Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Park_(Pittsburgh)) calls the Lake Trail a 5-mile loop shared by walkers, joggers, and cyclists. It follows the roads around the lake in Allison Park. This is the loop people use when they want a real long run without drawing one. Two laps is 10.

## Highland Park reservoir

In May 2025, the [Pittsburgh Post-Gazette](https://www.post-gazette.com/news/health/2025/05/05/pittsburgh-walking-tours/stories/202504250084) described the Highland Park reservoir as about a 1-mile loop of wide, flat trails, with shade and a view of the water. Alana Wenk of the Pittsburgh Parks Conservancy called that loop relaxing and level. Good for easy laps. Not a destination long run unless you repeat it.

## Schenley Park

[Pittwire](https://www.pittwire.pitt.edu/pittwire/features-articles/runners-delight) lists Schenley Park as one of the courses the Pitt cross country teams train on, along with the Montour Trail, North Park, and the Three Rivers Heritage Trail. The city says the Eliza Furnace Trail connects Schenley Park trails with Downtown. We are not putting a single loop distance on this one. The park is big enough to build the run you want, and the Jail Trail is the exit toward the river.

## Frick Park

Same Pittwire piece. Zach Lefever, then with the Pitt team, said, "If you want to have a hard day, you go there." Dirt, hills, trees. Save it for the day you want your legs to feel it. Do not treat it like the Montour.

## How to save one on Strava

Open Maps, switch the sport to Run, and search the trail name. Star a segment if you want it on your watch. For the Saturday route, use the link above instead of drawing a new one. Ours is already measured.

Strava's own help says Segment Explore shows a sampling of popular segments, and that sample depends on zoom. A fast time on a segment is not the same thing as a good place to meet a group.

If you want the flat one with other people, that is Saturday. 7:00 AM, 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray. [RSVP on the homepage](/).

## FAQs

**Does Strava publish a top 10 of Pittsburgh running routes?**
No. There is no public city ranking by athlete count. This list is the routes local trail pages and our own Saturday route actually describe, checked on November 17, 2026.

**Which of these is the GRYT Saturday run?**
The first one. 4.2 miles out and back on the Montour/Peters Trail from 212 Valley Brook Road in McMurray. We do not meet on the North Shore or in Frick Park.

**Can I walk the Saturday route?**
Yes. The run is no-drop. Walk, jog, or run. You can turn around early and still finish with the group.

**Is the Three Rivers Heritage Trail one loop?**
No. It is a set of riverfront segments. DCNR lists the system at 25.93 miles. The runnable pieces are shorter: about 3 miles on the North Shore toward Millvale, and about 5 miles on the South Side from Station Square.
