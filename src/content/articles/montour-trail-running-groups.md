If you want to run the Montour Trail with other people, here's the short answer. As of November 10, 2026, [GRYT Run Club](/) is the only weekly group run we could find on a public schedule that starts on the Montour Trail itself. It meets every Saturday at 7:00 AM at 212 Valley Brook Road in McMurray and runs the paved Peters Township section. It's free, no-drop, and open to walkers, joggers, and runners.

Pittsburgh's bigger clubs meet in the city or at rotating businesses, and the Montour Trail Council's own calendar is mostly races, walks, and trail work. This guide lists who runs on and near the trail, with days, costs, and where each detail comes from. If your group runs the Montour every week and isn't here, [tell us](mailto:nick@gryt.club) and we'll add it.

## GRYT Run Club: The Weekly Run on the Trail

[GRYT Run Club](/) is a free community run club in Pittsburgh's South Hills, founded by Laura and Nick Merich. It does one thing on purpose: the same run, at the same place, every Saturday.

- **When:** Every Saturday at 7:00 AM
- **Where:** 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray, in Peters Township (Washington County). It's a free gravel lot. Meet by the trail map board.
- **Route:** About 4 miles out and back on the Montour/Peters Trail, which is flat and paved here. You can turn around whenever you want.
- **Pace:** No-drop. The run ends when the last person finishes, and walking counts.
- **Cost:** Free, with no membership required. First-timers RSVP on grytrunclub.com and sign a short waiver.
- **After:** Coffee in the parking lot.

GRYT also tracks showing up instead of speed. The [Milestone Club](/milestones) recognizes 5, 10, 25, 50, and 100 Saturdays. For a full walkthrough of the morning, read [what to expect on a Saturday run](/resources/playbooks/saturday-runs-what-to-expect).

## Clubs That Run Near the Trail

These groups are close to the Montour Trail or popular with South Hills runners. None of them lists a standing weekly run on the trail, so check each club's calendar if trail miles are what you're after.

### City of Bridges Run Club

City of Bridges (CBRC) is an all-volunteer nonprofit with runs most days of the week. Its [group run calendar](https://www.cityofbridgesrunclub.com/group-runs) lists a South Hills Group Run every Thursday at 6:30 PM with 3 and 5 mile options, and the [club FAQ](https://www.cityofbridgesrunclub.com/cbrc-faqs) says that run includes a dedicated run/walk leader. Recent starts include Mt. Lebanon, Castle Shannon, Brookline, and Carnegie. A Tuesday 6:30 PM run rotates around the Robinson and Sewickley area. Runs are free and open to all, the club asks you to register, and membership is an optional $55 a year. The calendar names the business where each run starts, not the route, so ask the club if you want to know whether a given week touches the trail.

### Greater Pittsburgh Road Runners Club

The [Greater Pittsburgh Road Runners Club](http://gprrc.com/schedule-of-events/) has hosted monthly runs since 1974, many of them in South Park. Its 2026 schedule put one on the Montour Trail: the Cake Run on Sunday, June 21, with 2 mile and 5K options. Events cost $1 for members and $5 for non-members.

### Canonsburg Run Club

Canonsburg Run Club is a free weekly group for all ages and fitness levels. According to the [Observer-Reporter](https://www.observer-reporter.com/living/2026/oct/09/canonsburg-run-club-bringing-people-together-through-fitness/), it meets every Monday at 6:00 PM in the parking lot across from The Spineasy Cycle Lounge and heads out on a one-mile loop, with the option to add two more miles.

### Pittsburgh Rogue Runners & Walkers

[Pittsburgh Rogue Runners & Walkers](https://www.pittsburghroguerunners.com/faq-1) specializes in run/walk intervals and has a walker division. Its [RRCA listing](https://www.rrca.org/club/pittsburgh-rogue-runners-walkers/) is in Bethel Park, and it meets at rotating coffee shops and breweries around the Pittsburgh area, with the schedule posted in a weekly newsletter and on social media. You can try one event, and after that the club asks you to become a member.

### Fleet Feet Pittsburgh

Fleet Feet has a South Hills store at 1751 N. Highland Road and a free run club. Its [Run With Us page](https://www.fleetfeet.com/s/pittsburgh/training) currently lists weeknight runs in the East End and a Sunday 7:30 AM long run from rotating locations, with no standing run on the Montour Trail. The store does name the trail on its [list of favorite places to run](https://www.fleetfeet.com/s/pittsburgh/fleet-feet-flyers-run-club/where-to-run) south of the city, and it says an updated run club schedule is coming.

### One More to Ask About

20 Milers of Canonsburg appears in P3R's [MOVE Together club directory](https://www.thepittsburghmarathon.com/pages/rcr) with a Facebook page as its contact. We couldn't find a public schedule to check, so reach out to them directly.

## Races and Walks on the Montour Trail

If you'd rather show up for an event than a weekly run, the Montour Trail Council lists these [signature events](https://montourtrail.org/events/signature-events/) each year:

- **National Tunnel 10K/5K:** Cecil Township, late April. Held every spring since 2000.
- **J.R. Taylor Memorial Race/Walk:** A 5K run and fun walk on the Bethel Park Branch, late May or early June.
- **Peters Friends Twilight Walk:** An evening walk on the trail in McMurray, the last Friday in July.

Close to GRYT's meeting spot, [The Gratitude Run](https://www.thegratituderun.org/) is a Thanksgiving morning 5K at 9:00 AM from Peterswood Park. GRYT's [races page](/races) keeps verified dates for it and other Pittsburgh races.

One planning note for long runs: the National Tunnel in Cecil Township, at mile marker 25, has been closed since November 2025 because of a large crack in its roof, and [CBS Pittsburgh](https://www.cbsnews.com/pittsburgh/news/kd-sunday-spotlight-montour-trail-council-national-tunnel-repairs/) reports there's no safe detour around it. The Trail Council hopes to reopen it in fall 2027, according to the [Observer-Reporter](https://www.observer-reporter.com/news/local-news/2026/sep/09/montour-trail-making-progress-with-national-tunnel-fundraising-efforts/). That closure is several miles west of where GRYT meets in Peters Township.

## Why the Montour Trail Works for Group Runs

The [Montour Trail Council](https://montourtrail.org/) calls it the country's longest suburban rail-trail. The main line runs about 47 miles from Coraopolis to Clairton, and branches push the total past 60. Because it's an old railroad corridor, it's relatively flat.

That matters for a group. On a flat out-and-back, a walker and a marathoner can start together, turn around at different points, and still finish in the same parking lot. In Peters Township, the section known as the [Arrowhead Trail](https://www.peterstownship.com/Facilities/Facility/Details/Arrowhead-Trail-7) is 3.5 miles of paved surface from Route 19 to Brush Run Road, which makes it a good fit for strollers and wet days too.

For surfaces, trailheads, and the other South Hills paths, see our guide to [the best rail trails in the South Hills](/resources/articles/south-hills-rail-trails-running).

## FAQs

**What running groups meet on the Montour Trail?**
GRYT Run Club meets on the Montour Trail every Saturday at 7:00 AM at 212 Valley Brook Road in McMurray. As of November 10, 2026, it's the only weekly group run we could find on a public schedule that starts on the trail. The Greater Pittsburgh Road Runners Club held one 2026 event there, and other nearby clubs start from rotating businesses.

**Is there a free run club on the Montour Trail?**
Yes. GRYT Run Club is free, with no membership required. First-timers RSVP on grytrunclub.com and sign a short waiver.

**Is the Montour Trail good for beginners?**
Yes. It's relatively flat, and an out-and-back route lets you turn around whenever you want. GRYT's Saturday run is no-drop and welcomes walkers, so a first-timer can cover one mile or four.

**Where do I park for a group run on the Montour Trail in McMurray?**
GRYT meets in the free gravel lot at 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray, PA 15317. Meet by the trail map board a few minutes before 7:00 AM.

**Does City of Bridges Run Club run on the Montour Trail?**
Its calendar doesn't name the trail. City of Bridges lists a Thursday 6:30 PM South Hills Group Run and a Tuesday 6:30 PM Robinson-area run, both starting from rotating businesses.

**Are there races on the Montour Trail?**
Yes. The Montour Trail Council lists the National Tunnel 10K/5K in Cecil Township each spring and the J.R. Taylor Memorial 5K on the Bethel Park Branch in late May or early June. The Gratitude Run is a Thanksgiving morning 5K that starts at Peterswood Park in Peters Township.

**Is the whole Montour Trail open?**
Not right now. The National Tunnel in Cecil Township has been closed since November 2025 because of a crack in its roof, with no safe detour. Check the Montour Trail Council's site before planning a long run through that section.
