The person you want to bring is not deciding whether they like running. They are deciding whether they will be the only new one in the lot.

Your job is to remove that question before 7:00 AM. Not with a speech. With a time, a pin, and a plan for the first 10 minutes.

## What to send them

Send this, in your own words if you want, but do not leave out the practical lines.

Saturday, 7:00 AM. 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray. Free lot. I will be by the trail sign at 6:50.

It is about 4 miles out and back if we do the usual route, and you can turn sooner. Walk, jog, or run. Nobody is timing you. Coffee in the lot after.

If it is your first time, RSVP and sign the short waiver here: https://grytrunclub.com

That link has to be a real link they can tap. Do not say "I'll send the details later." Later is how people stay home.

## What you do at the lot

Come at 6:50, not 7:00. The FAQ tells first-timers to come 10 minutes early so someone can say hey. If you invited them, that someone is you.

Park in the free lot. Meet by the trail sign, not at a coffee shop down the road and not "somewhere on the trail." The group is visible. Point at it.

If they have not signed the waiver, do that on a phone before the start. It takes under a minute, and they only sign it once. A founder or a regular is there to find first-timers. You do not have to perform an introduction. You do have to stay next to them until they know one other face.

Tell someone at sign-in that they are new if you want a pair. Then pick a turnaround together. One mile out is a complete Saturday. So is the bridge. Decide before you move, so they are not negotiating it up a hill they did not expect. There is no hill like that on this section. There is still the feeling of not knowing. Kill that feeling in the lot.

## What not to do

Do not sell the club. Do not list pace groups you do not have. Do not promise a crowd, a playlist, or a person who is not coming.

Do not tell them it is "only" 4 miles if they have not run in a year. Tell them the turnaround is theirs. The [distance guide](/resources/articles/saturday-distance-from-mcmurray) is the thing to send if they ask how far.

Do not vanish at mile 1 to run your own workout. You invited them. Finish when they finish, or loop back so they are not alone on the return. That is the no-drop rule anyway. Faster people come back. The run ends when the last person finishes.

Do not skip the lot after. Coffee, water, and sometimes fruit are there. Ten minutes is enough. That is where a first-timer stops being a guest.

## The one ask

Bring one person. Not a group chat of twelve. One person you will actually wait for at 6:50.

If they cannot make this Saturday, the next one is the same lot and the same time. The calendar does not move. Saturday, November 21, is GRYT Run #29 at 7:00 AM. Saturday, November 28, is the same place. The monthly meetup is Saturday, December 5, at 7:00 AM in McMurray, if the bigger morning is an easier invite.

The weekly run is still the weekly run. December 5 is the morning with a simpler sentence: come with me on the 5th.

## If they bail the night before

Reply once. Same time next Saturday. Same lot. Do not stack three follow-ups. The point of a standing run is that missing one does not end it.

If they bail at 6:40 AM, go anyway. Tell them you will be there next week and you will wait at the sign at 6:50. Then run your own turnaround. A club that only happens when every invite lands is not a club.

## The day-before text

Send it the night before, not the morning of.

Tomorrow, 6:50 AM, trail sign at 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray. I will wait there. We can turn at a mile. Waiver is on https://grytrunclub.com if you have not done it.

That is enough. A paragraph about the mission does not help them find the lot.

## If they are nervous about being slow

Read them the FAQ line. If they can walk for 30 minutes, they can do a Saturday. Walk-run is welcome. Nobody is timing them.

You can also tell them the truth about the trail. It is flat. It is shaded. It is out and back, so turning around is the plan, not a failure.

What you should not say is that everyone is fast, or that everyone is a beginner. Both are lies. The lot has both. The format holds them.

## FAQs

**Do they have to RSVP if I am bringing them?**
Yes, if it is their first time. RSVP and the short waiver are how a founder or regular knows to look for them. The waiver is once, under a minute.

**What if they want to turn around early?**
That is a normal Saturday. Pick the turnaround in the lot. You can still finish together.

**Where do I send them?**
https://grytrunclub.com for the RSVP and waiver. The pin is 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray, PA 15317.
