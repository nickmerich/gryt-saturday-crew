November in McMurray is not a different club. It is the same lot, earlier dark, and a colder first mile.

The Saturday run still starts at 7:00 AM at 212 Valley Brook Road. The route is still the Montour / Peters Trail, out and back. The rules do not get stricter because the calendar turned. They get more specific.

This is the winter version of those rules, from the [FAQ](/faq) and the trail pages, not from a generic cold-weather list.

## We still run

The FAQ answer on weather is two words: we run.

The trail is mostly crushed limestone under a tree canopy, so it drains fast. A normal rainy Saturday is a regular Saturday. We only cancel for lightning, or for ice that makes the trail dangerous. That call goes up on Instagram or Strava the morning of. Do not assume a cancellation from a radar screenshot the night before.

If you are deciding at 6:15 AM whether to leave the house, check those two places, then come. The lot is the same.

## The first mile is the dark one

In winter, a headlamp helps for the first mile or two before the sun is up. That is the FAQ line, and it is the one people skip.

7:00 AM in late November is not a daylight start. The paved Peters section is shaded. A light on your head, or a bright layer, is the difference between seeing the edge of the trail and hoping. You do not need a race kit. You need to see the person in front of you and the trail under your feet.

Layers are the other half. Wear what you would normally run in, plus layers if it is cold. If you feel slightly cold standing in the lot at 6:55, you are close. You warm up once you move. A huge coat you have to carry by mile 2 is the usual miss.

The [what to wear tool](/resources/what-to-wear-running) will build a list from the Saturday forecast if you want the piece-by-piece version.

## Pavement first, limestone if you keep going

The saved Saturday route is 4.2 miles and 81 feet, out and back, on the paved Peters section. That is the winter-friendly piece. It is not the whole trail.

[TrailLink](https://www.traillink.com/trail/montour-trail/) describes the Montour as primarily crushed limestone, with smaller paved sections in Peters Township and Clairton. [Peters Township](https://www.peterstownship.com/Facilities/Facility/Details/Arrowhead-Trail-7) calls the Arrowhead section 3.5 miles of paved trail from Route 19 to Brush Run Road.

Limestone drains, which is why a rain day is usually still a run day. Ice is different. Packed ice on limestone, or a glazed bridge, is the thing that can cancel the morning. If you are going past the paved section on your own, treat the surface as a decision, not a surprise. Turn around when the footing changes if you came for an easy Saturday.

We do not publish a snowplow schedule because we do not have one we can stand behind. The morning-of call is the schedule.

## Water, time, and the lot after

Bring water for anything longer than 45 minutes. Cold does not remove that. People forget bottles in November because they are thinking about gloves.

The group goes out together and meets back at the trailhead. Coffee, water, and sometimes fruit are in the lot after. In the cold, those 10 minutes matter more, not less. A dry layer in the car is a better idea than standing in a wet shirt to be polite.

First-timers still RSVP and sign the short waiver once. Come 10 minutes early. If you are new, say so at sign-in and someone will pair you.

## What this is not

This is not a gear catalog. It is not a promise that every mile of the Montour is clear. It is the operating note for one lot, one time, and one trailhead.

If you want the longer system, read the tunnel note before you drive out for a point-to-point. The [Montour Trail Council](https://montourtrail.org/) says the National Tunnel at mile marker 25 is closed indefinitely after a late-2025 inspection found deteriorated concrete in the ceiling. That closure is not on the Saturday route out of Valley Brook. It matters if you were planning to pass through the tunnel on a long day. The council does not recommend an on-road detour, because of narrow lanes and limited sight lines. Westbound access is the McConnell Road trailhead. Eastbound access is Kurnick.

## Wet limestone is not ice

People cancel themselves for the wrong surface.

Wet crushed limestone, under trees, after a normal rain, is what the FAQ is describing when it says the trail drains fast. It can look darker than pavement. It is still usually a run day. Ice is a sheet on the trail, a glazed bridge deck, or footing that makes a walk dangerous. That is the cancel. If you are unsure at the lot, walk the first hundred yards. If it is just wet, go. If it is ice, turn around and check the morning-of post before you decide the day is cancelled for everyone.

The paved Peters section is the more predictable piece. If you came for an easy Saturday and the limestone past the pavement looks worse, turn on the pavement. You do not get a better story for pushing into bad footing.

## What to leave in the car

A dry layer. A plastic bag for a wet hat. Your keys where you can find them with cold hands. None of that is a rule. It is the difference between staying 10 minutes and sprinting to the driver's seat.

Gloves you can take off and pocket beat a pair you have to carry like a purse. A headlamp you tested the night before beats a dead one at 6:58. That is the whole winter kit that matters for this lot.

## A winter Saturday, in order

1. Check Instagram or Strava if the forecast looks like ice or lightning.
2. Headlamp or a light. Layers. Water if you will be out past 45 minutes.
3. 6:50 AM at 212 Valley Brook Road. Free lot. Trail sign.
4. Pick a turnaround before you start. The [distance guide](/resources/articles/saturday-distance-from-mcmurray) has the splits.
5. Finish at the sign. Change if you are wet. Stay for coffee if you can.

## FAQs

**Do you cancel for rain?**
No. Rain is a normal Saturday. The limestone drains. We cancel for lightning, or for ice that makes the trail dangerous.

**Where is the weather call posted?**
Instagram or Strava, the morning of. Not the night before as a final answer.

**Do I need a headlamp in November?**
For the first mile or two, yes, if you are starting at 7:00 AM before the sun is up. That is the FAQ guidance.

**Is the National Tunnel on the Saturday route?**
No. The Saturday run leaves from Valley Brook Road in McMurray. The closed tunnel is at mile marker 25, well away from that lot.
