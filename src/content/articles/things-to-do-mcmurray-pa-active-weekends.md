The best things to do in McMurray, PA on an active weekend start on the Montour Trail and Peters Lake Park. Add Boyce Mayview Park hiking, Simmons Farm, the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum, and Escape Room South for indoor or family time. Pick one major activity per afternoon and pair it with local food.

McMurray sits in Peters Township, and it makes a good base for a weekend outdoors. You get a rail-trail corridor, a reservoir loop, a large park with ten miles of trails, a working farm, and two indoor backups for rain. This guide covers the trail, park, farm, and indoor options, from a Saturday morning group run to an escape room, and shows you how to pace them so the weekend doesn't turn into a checklist. ## Why Is the Montour Trail the Best Place to Start?

The Montour Trail is the backbone of an active McMurray weekend. It handles walking, running, and biking on one continuous surface, and it passes right through town. You don't need a plan or a permit. You park, you start moving.

The trail is a multi-use rail-trail, so the grade is gentle and the surface is predictable. That matters if your crew includes a stroller, a new runner, and someone on a bike. Everyone shares the same path at their own speed.

The trail also has tunnels, which give it more character than a typical flat path. The National Tunnel is the longest on the Montour Trail at 623 feet, according to Komoot's guide to attractions around McMurray. The Greer Tunnel is another one locals mention. In winter, the National Tunnel can have ice buildup, though it stays accessible through its doorways, so check conditions before you commit to a cold-weather run through it.

The trail is part of something much bigger. Once completed, the Montour Trail will span nearly 47 miles and connect to a 400-mile trail network, according to [Patch's 2025 guide to hikes near Peters](https://patch.com/pennsylvania/peters/6-can-t-miss-hikes-near-peters). Today you can use the stretch near McMurray without worrying about the rest. If you like it, you have plenty of extra miles to grow into.

Start here for one reason: it costs nothing and asks nothing. Everything else on this list gets easier once you've moved your body.

## Things to Do in McMurray, PA This Weekend: Start With a Saturday Group Run

If you want one of the best things to do in McMurray, PA this weekend, start Saturday at 7 AM with a group run. [GRYT Run Club](https://grytrunclub.com/) is a free, in-person running group that meets every Saturday at 7 AM on the Montour/Peters Trail in McMurray, PA.

You show up, and you run or walk about 4 miles with the crew. All paces are welcome. Walkers, joggers, and runners start together, and nobody gets left behind. When you finish, the group grabs coffee in the parking lot.

There are no fees, no training plans, and no membership requirements. It costs nothing to try. Come once, skip three weeks, and come back. Nobody will ask where you've been.

This run is the right anchor for a weekend because it fixes your Saturday morning in place. You finish by 8:30 or so, you've had coffee with people who live nearby, and you still have the whole day ahead. You've also covered a stretch of the Montour Trail, the trail the rest of this guide keeps circling back to.

A morning run also stops the day from drifting. Without it, a Saturday of "we'll figure it out" becomes a late breakfast and a short list of things you didn't do. With it, you've done the hard part before 9 AM.

If you've never run with a group, the Saturday runs guide covers [what to expect on the Montour and Peters trails](https://grytrunclub.com/resources/playbooks/saturday-runs-what-to-expect). Pick one location. Pick one time. Show up. If you're new to the area, the Montour/Peters Trail is a friendly place to start, since the surface is smooth and the route is easy to follow. Bring water, wear shoes you've already broken in, and plan to stick around for coffee afterward, because that's where most of the conversation happens.

## Is Peters Lake Park Good for an Easy Walk or Fishing?

Yes. Peters Lake Park is one of the easiest outings in Peters Township, with a scenic loop around a reservoir that works for walking, fishing, and strollers.

The Peters Lake Trail is a 1.7-mile loop that suits easy strolls, according to Patch's 2025 list of hikes near Peters. You can finish it in under an hour at a walking pace, even with stops. It's flat enough that you don't need trail shoes or a plan.

The park suits families with small children who can't yet handle wooded or natural-surface trails. A loop around water keeps kids interested, and a loop means you never have to turn around halfway. If someone gets tired, you're never far from the car.

Fishing is the other draw. Bring a rod and the reservoir gives you a reason to slow down and stay a while. That makes this a good second outing after a morning run: your legs have done their work, and now you can move at the pace of the person with the smallest steps.

Pack a small snack and a water bottle, and give yourself time to stop at the water's edge. Think of Peters Lake Park as the relaxed half of your weekend. The Montour Trail asks for effort. This loop asks for an hour and a snack. Use it on the day you want something easy, or as the afternoon outing after Saturday's run.

## Where Can You Hike Near McMurray? Boyce Mayview Park

If you want more distance than the Peters Lake loop, Boyce Mayview Park is the next step up. It spans more than four hundred acres and has approximately ten miles of natural trails, according to [Petite Magnolia's 2026 fall guide to the South Hills](https://petitemagnolia.com/fall-activities-south-hills-pittsburgh/).

Most weekend lists skip it, so you can often have stretches of trail mostly to yourself on a Saturday afternoon. You trade the flat rail-trail surface for natural paths, so wear shoes with real grip. After rain, roots and mud come with the territory.

Ten miles of trail gives you room to choose. Walk a short loop for an hour with kids, or string together a longer hike if your legs still have energy. Nothing forces a commitment up front.

The park is a trail destination, not a food stop, so eat a full meal before you head out [S4].

Slot this on the day you want a real outdoor effort. It fits the one-major-activity-per-afternoon rule [S6]: if you hike Boyce Mayview, let that be the afternoon, and keep the rest of the day loose.

## Simmons Farm: Seasonal Fun for Families

Simmons Farm is the family stop on this list. It offers seasonal pick-your-own fruit, vegetables, and flowers, along with hayrides, a petting zoo, corn mazes, and a farm market.

The key word is seasonal. What's open depends on the time of year, so check the farm's current offerings before you drive out. Admission prices are generally not posted online and can change from year to year, so don't count on a number you saw elsewhere.

Two tips make the visit easier. First, feed the kids a full meal before you arrive [S4]. Farm snacks are tempting, and a hungry child makes the day short. Second, bring a wagon [S5]. It carries heavy bags of picked produce and tired small children across the property, and you'll use it more than you expect.

Pick your timing with care. Saturday afternoons in mid-October can be extremely crowded. Go earlier in the day or on a different day of the weekend, and you'll spend more time picking and less time waiting.

This is the right pick for the afternoon after a morning run. You've already moved, so the farm becomes a slow, social outing instead of another workout. Make the farm your one major activity that day [S6], and head home with something you picked yourself.

## What Are the Best Indoor Options When the Weather Turns?

Rain doesn't have to cancel the weekend. Near McMurray, the two best indoor backups are the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum and Escape Room South.

The Pennsylvania Trolley Museum preserves the trolley era, and it maintains a collection of more than 50 restored antique trolleys for visitors to explore, according to [Visit Washington County's 2025 indoor activities guide](https://www.visitwashingtoncountypa.com/blog/post/your-rain-snow-playbook-best-indoor-kids-activities/). Beyond the streetcars, it has interactive exhibits, and visitors can operate a car. That hands-on part is what makes it work for kids and adults who would rather do than look.

Hours are seasonal and vary significantly between summer and winter months, so confirm before you go. The museum also offers sensory-friendly hours, with a quieter environment and softer lighting. If someone in your group is sensitive to noise or bright light, ask about those sessions.

Escape Room South is the better choice for a group. It offers themed escape rooms at varying difficulty levels, so you can match the challenge to who's playing. It is open by appointment only on weekdays, which means you should book ahead instead of walking in.

Admission prices for both are often not listed online. Call or check each website before you leave so the cost doesn't surprise you.

Treat these as true backups, not afterthoughts. When the weather turns, pick one, and let it be the afternoon [S6].

## FAQs

McMurray is a community in Peters Township, in Washington County, southwest of Pittsburgh. It sits close to Canonsburg, PA, which makes it an easy base for the Montour Trail, Peters Lake Park, and the other spots in this guide. Check directions before you leave, since trailhead parking can be hard to spot on a map.

### What events are happening in Washington County, PA?

Events change by season, and we don't have a current calendar to share. Visit Washington County's website lists upcoming happenings, and local park and farm pages post seasonal events. ### What are some hidden gems in western Pennsylvania?

Boyce Mayview Park is a good one. It covers more than four hundred acres with about ten miles of natural trails, and it gets far less attention than the bigger regional parks, so you can often find quiet stretches on a Saturday afternoon. The Montour Trail's tunnels are another lesser-known draw, and the National Tunnel is the longest on the trail at 623 feet.

We don't have a ranking to cite, and it depends on who you ask. For an active weekend near McMurray, the Montour Trail is the strongest starting point, and the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum, with more than 50 restored antique trolleys, is a standout indoor stop.

### How much do the attractions in McMurray cost?

It varies. The Saturday group run is free, and trails and parks are generally low-cost outings. Admission prices for Simmons Farm, the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum, and Escape Room South are often not listed online, so check each website or call before you go.

### Can you get to McMurray attractions without a car?

Probably not easily. Public transport options to these locations are limited or unclear, and the attractions sit spread out across the area. Plan on driving or carpooling, and look up directions ahead of time so you aren't searching for parking on arrival.

Trails stay usable, though the National Tunnel on the Montour Trail may have ice buildup. The Pennsylvania Trolley Museum and Escape Room South work well indoors, but check their hours first, since the museum's schedule shifts between summer and winter. Escape Room South is open by appointment on weekdays.
