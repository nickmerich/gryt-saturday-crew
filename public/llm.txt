# GRYT Run Club
> Free Saturday morning run club on the Montour / Peters Trail in McMurray, PA.

## About
GRYT Run Club meets every Saturday at 7:00 AM at 212 Valley Brook Road, McMurray, PA, in Pittsburgh's South Hills. About 4 miles out and back on a flat, paved trail. All paces welcome, walkers included, and no one gets dropped. Free, with no membership required. First-timers RSVP on the site and sign a short liability waiver.
Location: McMurray, PA (Peters Township, Washington County), Montour / Peters Trail
